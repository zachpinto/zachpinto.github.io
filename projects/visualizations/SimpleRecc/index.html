    Data settings

    Live mode uses three free sources: TMDB for titles, posters, keywords and streaming availability; OMDb for Rotten Tomatoes and IMDb scores; and Wikidata for award winners. Keys are saved only in this browser.

    Get keys at themoviedb.org and omdbapi.com. Without a TMDB key, the site runs on a small built-in demo catalog.

    Titles, posters, keywords and streaming availability come from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming data on TMDB is supplied by JustWatch. Rotten Tomatoes and IMDb scores come from OMDb; award winners come from Wikidata.

    The recommendation model is deliberately simple: a weighted blend of the score you chose, how many of your picked traits a title has, and the genres and keywords of titles you rate.
