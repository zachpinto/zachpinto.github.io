My Media Poll

WHAT IS THIS?

I am trying to see what type of media people consume, and in what broad categories of media consumption.

WHAT DO I DO?

For each of the 10 sections below, you will be given five entries. Please write in the top 5 genres OR specific pieces of content you consume in this category. Please note you do not have to write in all five entries. You can leave some or all blank if you don't consume much or any media in this category. You can make your entries as broad or as specific as you'd like. I will also give examples for each section.
